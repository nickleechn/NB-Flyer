# NB Fly Thumbnail Style

## Overall look

Bold, high-contrast travel and aviation thumbnails with an editorial, opinion-led feel. Each image should communicate one clear verdict, question, or comparison at a glance.

## Format

- 16:9 landscape canvas, ideally 1280 × 720 px.
- Design for legibility at small YouTube thumbnail size.
- Do not include browser chrome, YouTube interface elements, video duration badges, or progress bars.

## Composition

- Use one dominant location or transport image as the full-bleed background: aircraft, cabin, hotel room, skyline, or destination.
- Place a large cut-out portrait of the presenter in the foreground, usually occupying the right or lower-right third.
- Use an expressive but believable reaction or gesture: surprise, delight, concern, pointing, or contemplation.
- Keep the presenter and headline visually separate so neither obscures the other.
- Add subtle edge light, shadow, or contrast around the presenter to separate them from the background.
- Simplify or softly blur busy background areas behind the headline.

## Headline treatment

- Use a very short hook of roughly 3–6 words, arranged over 2–4 lines.
- Prefer sentence fragments that express tension or judgment, such as “Small but good location,” “Old but not obsolete,” or “Larger economy seat?”
- Make the main words large, bold, condensed, and slightly italicized.
- Use white for most words and warm golden yellow for the key contrast, benefit, or emotional phrase.
- Add a dark outline or soft black drop shadow for strong separation.
- Left-align the headline and vary line lengths to create a compact block.
- Optional legacy variation: a large editorial serif headline in white and gold, especially for premium-service or comparison stories.

## Colour and lighting

- Core palette: white, warm golden yellow, charcoal/black, with the natural blues and warm neutrals of the scene.
- Use saturated but realistic colour grading, bright faces, crisp skies, and warm cabin or hotel lighting.
- Maintain strong local contrast around the face, aircraft, and headline.
- Small brand colours or airline logos may appear as supporting details, never as the main focal point.

## Image character

- Photographic and polished rather than illustrated.
- Clean cut-outs, crisp facial detail, and a believable sense of place.
- Slightly heightened scale and contrast are welcome, but avoid surreal distortion or excessive effects.
- Aim for confident, curious, practical, and trustworthy—not sensational or chaotic.

## Reusable generation prompt

> Create a polished 16:9 YouTube thumbnail for a travel or aviation review. Use [LOCATION / AIRCRAFT / CABIN] as a full-bleed photographic background. Place a large, clean cut-out of [PRESENTER] in the [RIGHT / LOWER-RIGHT], showing [EXPRESSION OR GESTURE]. Add the short left-aligned headline “[HEADLINE]” over 2–4 lines in a bold condensed slightly italic sans-serif typeface. Set most words in white and emphasize “[KEY WORDS]” in warm golden yellow, with a subtle black outline and shadow. Use saturated but realistic colour, crisp facial detail, strong subject separation, and an uncluttered editorial composition. Keep all important elements inside safe margins. Do not add a YouTube interface, duration badge, progress bar, or unrelated text.

## Quality checklist

- One obvious subject and one obvious idea.
- Headline remains readable when viewed small.
- Key word or phrase is highlighted in yellow.
- Face and background tell the same story as the headline.
- No more than three major visual elements compete for attention.
- No platform UI or accidental text appears in the image.
